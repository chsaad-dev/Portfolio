Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy outline the data practices for the portfolio website of Muhammad Saad (https://muhammadsaad-portfolio.web.app/). It describes what information is collected, how it is managed, and the third-party integrations utilized on this site.

2. Information Collection & Form Submissions

If you choose to contact me via the contact form on this website, you will be asked to provide your name, email address, and a message. This data is collected solely for communication purposes and to discuss potential freelance or full-time Android development opportunities.

3. Cookies & Analytical Measurements

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to analyze web traffic, optimize site layout, and track user engagement. GA4 uses cookies—small text files stored on your local device—to capture anonymous statistics such as page views, scroll depth, external link clicks, and file download events. No personally identifiable information (PII) is stored inside these analytical cookies.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Services

To host this site and process contact queries, the following services are integrated:

  • Firebase Hosting: Hosts all static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and asset resources. Firebase may capture server access logs (e.g. IP addresses) for standard security auditing.
  • Web3Forms: Processes contact form submissions. Your name, email, and message text are securely forwarded through Web3Forms to deliver the inquiry directly to my email address.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Analyzes visitor traffic and user engagement as described in Section 3.

5. Data Retention & Security

Your messages submitted through the contact form are retained for as long as necessary to conduct project communication or recruitment processes. I implement industry-standard practices, including HTTPS encryption enforced on Firebase Hosting, to protect your submitted data.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or data-related inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via email: saaddevlabs@gmail.com.
